HOW IT WORKS
▶ NEW HERE? TAKE THE INTERACTIVE TOUR
A guided walkthrough of every feature — the city, zones, view modes, scanning, controls, and more. Skip anytime.
🏙️ The City
A GPU-accelerated real-time simulation of the AI industry. Buildings grow taller as labs release more models. New labs get dynamically zoned with HQs. The lab with the highest ELO gets a golden Apex Beacon. The cheapest API provider gets a green SALE sign. A Leaderboard Monument in the park tracks the #1 model. AI models walk the streets as living entities — they age (baby → kid → adult → retired), commute, eat, sleep, and have unique appearances based on their parameter count and architecture (MoE, dense, etc.).

🚢 Port District → 🏜️ Space Zone → 🌲 Frontier Pines → 🏠 Worker Housing → 🖥️ Compute District → 🏠 Residential → 🎓 University → 🌲 Pine Reserve → 🏛️ AI Court → 🏢 Tech District → 📝 Conference → 🚇 Metro East → 🍸 Neon Bar → 🌐 The Backbone → 🤖 Robotics Factory → 🧬 Longevity Wing → 🤖 Agent District → 🌲 Silicon Woods → 💰 VC Row → 🏡 Billionaire's Row → ⚡ Power Grid
🛰️ Scanning & Discovery
The AI-powered scanner finds 4 real models per scan using your API key. It uses weighted category selection (Big Tech 40%, Asian 15%, Open Source 15%) and gap analysis to prioritize underrepresented labs. It also discovers CEOs/founders for labs that don't have one yet, automatically creating their car, helicopter, and estate.
📡 Camera Tracking
Click any AI model or CEO and press 📡 Track to lock the camera on them. Watch them commute via metro, enter buildings, work at desks, go camping, or fly helicopters — the camera automatically enters and exits interiors to follow them. Drag to cancel.
🌌 3D Holomap
Click 🌍 Holomap to launch a full Three.js 3D galaxy where the entire AI industry orbits a central ASI singularity. Each lab is a glowing nebula — the biggest labs (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic) orbit closest to the black hole. Every AI model is a star inside its lab's cluster, sized by benchmarks. Orbit, zoom, and click any star for full stats.
🛰️ Orbit Mode
Pull the camera up past the sky boundary or click the 🛰️ Orbit button in the minimap to enter Low Earth Orbit. See the Earth's curvature with atmosphere glow, city lights on the dark side, and real satellite positions from CelesTrak — Starlink, OneWeb, ISS, GPS, and Galileo constellations.
🚀 Space Zone
Scroll left past the worker housing to find the desert biome with 7 launch pads (SpaceX, Blue Origin, NASA, CNSA, ESA, ULA, Rocket Lab), a Vehicle Assembly Building, Mission Control, and an Orbital Tracking Station. Rockets launch based on real-time launch data from the Launch Library 2 API.
🤖 Robotics Factory
The Physical AI Manufacturing District features 4 buildings: Assembly Line, Testing Ground, Deployment Dock, and R&D Lab. Watch walking robot prototypes, welding sparks, conveyor belts, and smoke puffs. Companies: Tesla Optimus, Figure, Boston Dynamics, Unitree, Agility, and more.
🧬 Longevity Research Wing
AI-powered drug discovery and life extension. 4 buildings: Drug Discovery Lab, Clinical Trials Center, Genomics Sequencing, and Cryonics Vault. Animated DNA double helix, molecule bubbles, heartbeat pulses, sequencer LEDs, and cryo vapor. Companies: Calico, Altos Labs, Insilico Medicine, Isomorphic Labs.
🤖 Agent District
The autonomous AI agent neighborhood — home to the next wave of AI: agentic systems that perform multi-step tasks. Browser agents, coding agents, research agents, and computer-use agents work in dedicated towers. Watch them spawn task threads in real time.
🧑‍💼 CEOs & Billionaire's Row
Each AI lab's CEO drives a luxury car between their HQ and private estate. On weekends, CEOs take helicopters to Silicon Woods — a luxury retreat with a whiskey bar, infinity hot tub, putting green, and glamping domes. New CEOs are auto-discovered via scan and get a mansion built on Billionaire's Row.
🌲 Forests & Wild Spaces
Pine Reserve (near residential) — AI models go camping, fishing, and picnicking on weekends. Silicon Woods — A billionaire CEO retreat with fire pits, zen gardens, and fairy-lit redwoods. Frontier Pines — separates the space zone from the city. All feature outdoor sky rendering matching the time of day.
🔬 X-Ray Mode
Click the 🔬 X-Ray button to toggle a diagnostic overlay. The city dims to reveal building wireframes with lab-colored outlines, ID labels, stat metadata, data flow packets traveling along connection arcs, a coordinate grid, and a sweeping scan line. A dark terminal/hacker aesthetic for data nerds.
📊 Dashboards & Tools
The top bar gives you 📊 Bench (full leaderboard), 👥 Census (every citizen), 🚀 Launches (rocket schedule), 🧬 Lineage (model family trees), 📰 News (AI headlines), 📅 Cal (event calendar), 🏭 Hardware (compute clusters), ⚡ Grid (power status), 💰 Costs (pricing dashboard), 🤖 Analyst (ask AI questions about the city), and 🏆 Trophies (achievements).
