HOW IT WORKS
🏙️ The City
A GPU-accelerated real-time simulation of the AI industry. Buildings grow taller as labs release more models. New labs get dynamically zoned with HQs. The lab with the highest ELO gets a golden Apex Beacon. The cheapest API provider gets a green SALE sign. A Leaderboard Monument in the park tracks the #1 model.
🚀 Space Zone
Scroll left past the residential area to find the desert biome with 7 launch pads (SpaceX, Blue Origin, NASA, CNSA, ESA, ULA, Rocket Lab), a Vehicle Assembly Building, Mission Control, and an Orbital Tracking Station. Rockets launch based on real-time launch data from the Launch Library 2 API. Click any facility to enter its interior.
🌌 3D Holomap
Click 🌍 Holomap to launch a full Three.js 3D galaxy where the entire AI industry orbits a central ASI singularity. Each lab is a glowing nebula — the biggest labs (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic) orbit closest to the black hole. Every AI model is a star inside its lab's cluster, sized by benchmarks. Orbit, zoom, and click any star for full stats. The frontier model per lab gets a labeled badge. Deep-space ambient audio with procedural cosmic pings. Click 🏙️ Street View to return to the city.
🧑‍💼 CEOs & Billionaire's Row
Each AI lab's CEO drives a luxury car between their HQ and private estate. On weekends, CEOs take helicopters to Silicon Woods — a luxury retreat with a whiskey bar, infinity hot tub, putting green, and glamping domes. New CEOs are auto-discovered via scan and get a mansion built on Billionaire's Row.
📡 Camera Tracking
Click any AI model or CEO and press 📡 Track to lock the camera on them. Watch them commute via metro, enter buildings, work at desks, go camping, or fly helicopters — the camera automatically enters and exits interiors to follow them. Drag to cancel.
🌲 Forests & Interiors
Pine Reserve (near residential) — AI models go camping, fishing, and picnicking on weekends. Silicon Woods (between tech and estates) — A billionaire CEO retreat with fire pits, zen gardens, and fairy-lit redwoods. Frontier Pines — separates the space zone from the city. All feature outdoor sky rendering matching the time of day.
🛰️ Scanning & Discovery
The AI-powered scanner finds 4 real models per scan using your API key. It uses weighted category selection (Big Tech 40%, Asian 15%, Open Source 15%) and gap analysis to prioritize underrepresented labs. It also discovers CEOs/founders for labs that don't have one yet, automatically creating their car, helicopter, and estate.
